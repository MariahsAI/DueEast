The kitchen

A chef on shore and a chef offshore

Breakfast before the boats, lunch wherever you happen to be floating, dinner at the house. Menus are built around what you catch and what came in on the mail boat that morning — hogfish, conch, spiny lobster in season, tuna within hours of the gaff.

Offshore service travels: raw bar on the sandbar, ceviche cut on the swim platform, grilled catch served on a beach nobody else is standing on. Tell us your restrictions once and you won't have to say it again.
